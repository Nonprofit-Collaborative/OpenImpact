# v0.4 draft release notes: donor levels

Draft lines for the v0.4 release notes, written for Maria rather than for developers
(plan Section 7.5). Feature G-14 (donor levels), the NPSP Levels successor.

- **Your own names for your donors.** Build a ladder of levels (Friend, Sustainer,
  Leadership Circle, whatever you call them), say what each one starts at, and Open Impact
  keeps every household, organization, and person on the right rung as their giving
  changes. Nobody runs a report and nobody updates a field.
- **Levels read the giving total you already have.** A level is a label on the total shown
  on the same record, so a donor's level can never disagree with the number printed next
  to it. Choose which total the ladder measures: total giving, which never resets and is
  the default, this fiscal year, last fiscal year, or the largest single gift.
- **Movement is visible.** Every donor record keeps the level they were on before and the
  date they moved, so "who joined the Leadership Circle this month" and "who dropped out
  of it" are both a list view away.
- **A refund moves a donor down, and says so.** The level always states what the current
  total earns. What is kept is the record of where the donor came from, not a level they
  no longer reach.
- **Households, organizations, and people all get a level**, each from their own giving
  total, because that is where the totals already sit.
- **Placed the moment a gift lands**, then re-placed by a nightly pass that catches your
  fiscal year turning over and any ladder you edited, and by a Recalculate now button when
  you do not want to wait for the night.
- **Two settings, both in the app**, in Nonprofit Settings under Giving: whether levels are
  on, and which total they are measured on. The Donor levels page in the same section shows
  the ladder, how many donors are on each rung, and when everybody was last placed.
- **Nothing is shipped for you to undo.** Open Impact ships no levels of its own, because
  the amounts belong to your organization. Retire a rung by clearing its Active box rather
  than deleting it, so the donors who reached it keep the name.
