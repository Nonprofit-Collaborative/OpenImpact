# v0.4 draft release notes: rollups arrive with the install

Draft lines for the v0.4 release notes, written for Maria rather than for developers
(plan Section 7.5). A defect fix in C-13 and G-02: the shipped rollup definitions were
never created, so a fresh install had no giving totals at all.

- **Your giving totals are there when you arrive.** Installing the Giving module creates
  every total it ships, switched on and ready to calculate: total giving, gift count,
  first and last gift, largest gift, this year and the two years before it, pledge
  balance, soft credits, what a fund and an appeal have raised, and what has been paid
  against a pledge. Until now they were created only by a button on the Rollups page, and
  nothing told you to press it, so an org whose administrator never opened that page had
  every one of those numbers blank.
- **An upgrade adds what is new and changes nothing else.** A rollup you edited, renamed,
  switched to another mode, or switched off stays exactly as you left it. New totals
  shipped by a later version appear alongside it.
- **Restore shipped rollups still does what it says**, and is now the recovery step rather
  than the first step: press it if the page is empty after an install, or if a rollup you
  need is missing. It creates only what is absent.
- **Say no to a total by clearing its Active box, not by deleting it.** A deleted rollup
  comes back at the next upgrade, because Open Impact cannot tell one you deleted on
  purpose from one that was never created. An inactive rollup stays inactive for good, and
  keeps the numbers it has already calculated.
- **Still yours to start: the nightly recalculation.** Scheduling it is one click on the
  Rollups page and nothing in the app does it for you.

Admin guide: `docs/admin-guide/rollups.md`. Decision: ADR-0029.
