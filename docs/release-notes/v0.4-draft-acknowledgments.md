# v0.4 draft release notes: acknowledgments

Draft lines for the v0.4 release notes, written for Maria rather than for developers
(plan Section 7.5). Feature G-12 (acknowledgment rules and templates with sent tracking).

- **Write your thank you rules once.** "Gifts over $1,000 get the director's letter,
  everything else gets the standard email, gifts to the staff campaign get nothing." Open
  Impact matches every gift as it is saved and puts it in the right queue. Rules match on
  amount, gift type, appeal, and whether it is the donor's first gift, and the first rule in
  your list that fits a gift wins.
- **Emails use ordinary Salesforce email templates.** Not a special editor of ours: the one
  you already have, with its preview, its test send, and its merge fields. Open Impact ships
  one thank you to start from and your rules can point at any template in your org.
- **Letters come out as a spreadsheet you merge.** One row per gift with the donor's name,
  your household greeting, the address, the amount and the date, ready for the letter you
  already have in Word or Google Docs. Print them, then press Mark as sent. Open Impact does
  not pretend to know whether anything came out of your printer.
- **Every thank you is recorded.** Which gift, which letter, to which address, on what date,
  and if it did not go, why. So "did we thank the Hendersons" and "which letter did they
  get" are both a click away, and a thank you that went twice reads as two records rather
  than as a mystery.
- **Nobody gets thanked twice by accident.** Not when a gift is edited, not when a refund is
  recorded, and not when a send that failed halfway is run again. If you genuinely want to
  thank a donor again, set the gift's acknowledgment status back to To acknowledge, and the
  second thank you is recorded beside the first.
- **Refunds are left alone.** Refunded gifts, written off gifts, pending gifts and the
  negative gifts that record a refund are never queued, and refunding a gift that was already
  thanked for does not withdraw the thank you.
- **One failed send never stops the others.** A bad address is recorded on its own record
  with the reason, written to the Error Log, and tried again on the next send. The rest of
  the batch goes out.
- **Send when you like, or overnight.** A Send emails now button on the Acknowledgments page,
  and a nightly send you can switch on from the same page. It is off until you turn it on,
  and you never open Setup to schedule it.
- **The page tells you what is waiting**: how many emails, how many letters, how many gifts
  no rule covers, and how many have nobody to email, so an incomplete set of rules does not
  look like an empty queue.

Admin guide: `docs/admin-guide/acknowledgments.md`. Decision: ADR-0029.

Known limits in this release, recorded honestly:

- Open Impact does not print letters. It produces the merge file; the letter and the printing
  are yours.
- A rule names its email template by developer name, typed in. The Acknowledgments page lists
  every template in your org with its developer name beside it, and flags a rule whose
  template it cannot find.
- Editing the wording of an email template happens in Salesforce Setup, under Email
  Templates, because that is where Salesforce keeps its own editor. Everything else about
  acknowledgments is in the app.
