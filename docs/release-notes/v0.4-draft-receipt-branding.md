# v0.4 draft release notes: receipt branding (v0.4 engineering row)

Draft lines for the v0.4 release notes, written for Maria rather than for developers (plan
Section 7.5). The engineering item "gift receipt PDF branding settings", built alongside G-18
because both are about what a receipt says.

- **Your documents carry your organization, whatever your letter says.** Your legal name and
  address print at the top of every receipt and statement, and your tax identification number
  at the bottom, even if you have edited the letterhead out of your template. If your letter
  does print the name itself, Open Impact does not print it twice.
- **Your logo, if you want it.** Upload it once in the Organization section of Nonprofit
  Settings, or in step five of the Setup Assistant, and it prints at the top of receipts and
  statements. Two settings in the Receipts section control it: whether it prints at all, and
  how wide it is in millimetres.
- **Everything is in the app.** All three settings, and the organization details themselves,
  are in the Nonprofit Settings console. Nothing here needs Salesforce Setup.
- **A receipt with no logo is a valid receipt.** That is not a consolation, it is the design:
  nothing about images is allowed to stop a document being produced. A logo that cannot be
  found produces a document without one, and an Error Log entry saying which file and why.

## What is not settled, and what to do about it

**Whether the logo prints at all is still an open question, and it needs a real org to
answer.** ADR-0016 recorded two spikes for the receipting work, and this is the second of
them: nobody has yet confirmed how an image reaches the Salesforce PDF rendering service. No
Apex in this repository has been executed and no org was available while this was built, so
the image path is implemented and **unverified**.

What that means for you, concretely:

1. Your receipts are correct and complete without a logo. Numbering, the tax sentences, the
   organization details and the signature block are all text, and none of them depends on this.
2. If your logo does not print, check first that the logo file is shared with the people who
   issue receipts. Open Impact will not print a file the running user cannot see, and it
   writes an Error Log warning saying so rather than failing the receipt.
3. If it still does not print, the **Logo delivery route** setting has the other two routes
   ADR-0016 names: `Embedded image` and `Document server`. Changing that setting is the whole
   fix if one of them turns out to work in your org, which is why it is a setting rather than
   something that would need a new version of the product.
4. If none of them works, turn **Print the logo on receipts** off, so your documents are
   deliberately plain rather than accidentally so, and tell us: the answer from one real org
   settles this for everybody and lets the setting be retired.

Admin guide: `docs/admin-guide/receipts.md`. Model rule: R-RC12. Decision: ADR-0016.
