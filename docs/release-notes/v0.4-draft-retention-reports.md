# v0.4 draft release notes: Retention reports, with the definitions written down (G-16, ADR-0025, ADR-0026)

Written for Maria, to be folded into `v0.4.md` at the end of the iteration (plan Sections
7.4 and 7.5).

- **Four retention reports ship in the Open Impact Giving folder.** LYBUNT Donors, SYBUNT
  Donors, New Versus Retained Donors, and First to Second Gift Conversion. Nothing to build
  and no filters to set: open the Fundraising app, choose Reports, and they are there next to
  the dashboard reports.
- **Each report says who it counts, and the admin guide says it in plain language.** A
  qualifying gift is a gift in status Received, Refunded or Written off for an amount above
  zero, which is the rule the donor rollups already follow. So a gift refunded later still
  counts as a gift, and a refund is never counted as one. A donor whose gift you refunded this
  year is not on the LYBUNT list, and a donor whose last year gift you refunded this year
  still is.
- **The lapsed lists have no hidden lookback.** LYBUNT is "their most recent qualifying gift
  fell in the last fiscal year". SYBUNT is "their most recent qualifying gift fell before this
  fiscal year began", reaching back to the first gift in the org, grouped by the year they
  lapsed so you can cut it where you like.
- **New, retained and reactivated are three different words, on purpose.** New means the first
  gift ever falls in this fiscal year. Retained means they gave this year and last year.
  Reactivated means they gave this year, first gave in an earlier year, and gave nothing last
  year. The New Versus Retained report shows all three, and the record count on a group is a
  count of donors rather than gifts.
- **Conversion is a cohort with a stated window.** Of the donors whose first gift fell in one
  of the two completed fiscal years, how many have given again between then and today.
  Compare a cohort with itself a year later, not with the cohort before it, because they have
  not had the same amount of time.
- **One setup step: make the two fiscal years agree.** Salesforce reports read the fiscal year
  in Setup, and Open Impact's rollups read the fiscal year start month in Nonprofit Settings.
  Set them to the same month, then recalculate the rollups. The admin guide's first setup step
  and its "Common mistakes" say what a mismatch looks like.
- **For builders: one new attribute, `Gifts_Last_Year__c` on Account and Contact.** A count of
  qualifying gifts in the previous fiscal year, filled by three new shipped rollup defaults.
  It exists because a sum could not answer the retained question honestly: a gift refunded
  inside last year sums to zero and the donor would have read as reactivated. Everything else
  the reports need was already on the donor record. There is also one new report type, Donors
  with Giving, whose rows are donors rather than gifts.
- **These reports have not been run in an org.** No report in this repository has. What has
  been checked is that the metadata is well formed and that each filter matches the definition
  on the [Retention reports page](../admin-guide/retention-reports.md). Spot check one donor
  from each list against their gift history before the first mailing.

Read [Retention reports](../admin-guide/retention-reports.md), and
[ADR-0026](../architecture/decisions/0026-retention-report-definitions.md) for why each
definition is what it is.
