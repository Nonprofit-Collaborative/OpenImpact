# v0.6 draft release notes: Campaign sync

Draft lines for the v0.6 release notes, written for Maria (plan Section 7.5). Feature X-02,
in the Connect module.

- **Your appeals, as Campaigns.** Switch on **Copy appeals to Campaigns** in Nonprofit
  Settings and every appeal gets a matching Salesforce Campaign with the same name, dates,
  goal and cost, so Campaign Members, email tools and Campaign reports work with the efforts
  you already track. Change the appeal and the Campaign follows; nothing flows back.
- **Catch up in one click.** The new Campaign sync page shows how many appeals have a
  Campaign and gives the rest theirs with **Sync all appeals**.
- **Nothing is deleted, nothing is blocked.** Deleting an appeal leaves its Campaign and its
  members. An appeal always saves, even when its Campaign cannot be made; the reason is in
  the Error Log.
- **Off until you turn it on,** so an organization that already has Campaigns decides first.
  Orgs without Campaigns (Salesforce Platform licenses) are told so on the page.
