# v0.3 draft release notes: Relationships and Affiliations (C-15, C-16)

Written for Maria, to be folded into `v0.3.md` at the end of the iteration (plan Sections 7.4
and 7.5).

- **Enter a relationship once, get both sides.** Record that Harper is Luis's spouse on
  Harper's record and Luis's record shows it too, immediately, without anyone typing it twice.
  The same goes for parents and children, siblings, grandparents, friends, and colleagues.
- **The two sides can never disagree.** Ending a relationship, retyping it, or writing a note
  on it changes both sides in the same save. Deleting one deletes the other, because they are
  one connection, not two records that happen to match.
- **None of the shipped types has a gender.** Parent becomes Child, Grandparent becomes
  Grandchild, and Spouse, Partner, Sibling, Friend, and Colleague all become themselves. No
  relationship ever asserts anything about a person's gender.
- **The dates decide the status.** Give a relationship an end date and both sides read Former.
  Clear it and both sides read Current again.
- **You can switch the mirroring off, for everything or for one record.** A new setting under
  Relationships in Nonprofit Settings turns off the automatic other side for the whole
  organization, and clearing Keep Both Sides In Step on a single relationship stops Open Impact
  synchronizing that one while leaving the pairing in place.
- **Affiliations connect a person to an organization without moving them.** Where someone
  works, which board they sit on, the congregation they belong to, each with a role and the
  dates it ran. Their household, their giving history, and their letters are untouched: that is
  the mistake this record exists to prevent.
- **One primary organization per person, kept for you.** Mark an affiliation primary and the
  previous one stops being primary in the same save, and the person's Primary Affiliation field
  names the organization, so a list view or a mail merge can show an employer without a report.
- **A connection that has ended is never the primary one.** Setting an end date takes the
  primary badge off and empties the person's Primary Affiliation field, because a field naming
  an employer someone left last year is worse than an empty one.
- **Two terms on the same board are two records.** Open Impact refuses a second affiliation for
  the same person, organization, and role whose dates overlap one already on file, and names
  the record to open instead.
- **New cards and new tabs.** A Relationships card and an Affiliations card on every person's
  record, an Affiliations card on every organization's record showing the people connected to
  it, and Relationships and Affiliations tabs in the Nonprofit Hub app for reporting and list
  views.
- **Both work whether your people are contacts or person accounts.** A relationship between two
  people, or between a person and an organization, reads and writes the same whichever way your
  org holds people.
