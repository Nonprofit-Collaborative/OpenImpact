# Release note lines: households, names, and greetings (draft for v0.1)

Written for Maria, per plan Section 7.4. These three lines are assembled into the public
v0.1 release notes; they are not the iteration report.

- **Households are created for you.** Save a new person without a household and Open
  Impact makes one, names it, and attaches them. Move someone to another household and
  both member counts correct themselves, with the empty household tidied away if you want
  it to be.
- **Names and greetings that are already right.** Every household gets a name ("The Garcia
  Family"), a formal greeting for letters ("Mr. and Mrs. John Smith"), and an informal
  greeting for notes ("John and Jane"), recalculated whenever the people change, with
  people who have died left out of greetings.
- **Change the wording without a consultant.** Edit the naming patterns on the Nonprofit
  Settings page, watch five sample households change as you type, and click "Recompute all
  households" when you are happy. Any household marked "custom name" keeps the wording your
  team typed.
