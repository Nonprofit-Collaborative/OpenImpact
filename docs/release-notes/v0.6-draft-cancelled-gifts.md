# v0.6 draft release notes: cancelled gifts

Draft lines for the v0.6 release notes, written for Maria (plan Section 7.5). A change to
refunds and write-offs (G-04) in the Giving module, decided with the posting lock (G-20).

- **Cancel a gift that will never be paid.** A Pending gift, such as a pledge payment that never
  came, can now be set to **Cancelled**. Nothing else is created: it stays out of every total,
  the accounting export, receipts and acknowledgments, as it was while Pending.
- **It works in a closed month.** A Pending gift was never in the books, so cancelling it
  changes nothing your bookkeeper holds, even after the month is closed.
- **Installments are freed.** Cancelling a gift that was expected to pay a pledge installment
  frees the installment for the next gift.
- **Write-offs are for money you received.** Refunding or writing off a Pending gift is now
  refused, with a message saying to cancel it. The advice to delete an unpaid Pending gift is
  gone from the guides.
- **Changed your mind?** Set a Cancelled gift back to Pending. Every change to or from Cancelled
  is written to the Error Log with who made it.
