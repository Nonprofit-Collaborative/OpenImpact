# v0.4 draft release notes: households keep up with people held as person accounts

Draft lines for the v0.4 release notes, written for Maria rather than for developers
(plan Section 7.5). Feature C-01 (households), defect found by reading the person account
path against the contact path.

In an org that stores people as person accounts, Open Impact used to notice a person being
created, and nothing after that. These three lines close that gap. Nothing changes in an
org that stores people as contacts, where all three already worked.

- **Deleting a person updates the households they were in.** The member count comes down,
  the name and the greetings are written again without them, and a household nobody is left
  in is tidied away when your settings ask for that. Before, the household kept a count, a
  name and greetings that still included somebody who was gone.
- **Restoring a person from the Recycle Bin puts them back in their households.** Their
  membership comes back with them, and the count, the name and the greetings go back to
  including them. Before, a restored person came back belonging to nobody.
- **Changing a person changes what their household is called.** Renaming somebody, giving
  them a preferred name, marking them deceased, changing their household role, or ticking
  either of the "exclude from" boxes now rewrites the household name and greetings, exactly
  as it already did for a person held as a contact. Before, only clearing a household's own
  "custom name" box did that.

## Stated plainly, what this release does not do

- **A membership that was already broken is not repaired.** A person deleted before this
  release left a membership row behind that names nobody. Open Impact counts and names as
  though that row were not there, which it always did, but it does not go looking for those
  rows to remove them. Deleting them is yours to do, and safe to do.
