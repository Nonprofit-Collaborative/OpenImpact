# v0.4 draft release notes: Sample data covers Giving (C-10)

Written for Maria, to be folded into `v0.4.md` at the end of the iteration (plan Sections 7.4
and 7.5).

- **The sample data now has money in it.** Load Sample Data from Nonprofit Settings and, if
  you have Giving, you get about 700 gifts across three years, 8 funds, 7 appeals, 24 pledges
  and recurring gifts with their installments, 30 soft credits and 24 tributes, on top of the
  200 households, 440 people and 25 organizations that were already there.
- **It looks like a real donor list.** Donors who have given every year, donors who lapsed
  eighteen months ago, donors whose first gift arrived last month, a few major donors, and
  households who have never given. Most gifts are under 500 and a few are over 10,000, so the
  reports and the Giving Overview dashboard show a shape you will recognise.
- **The awkward cases are in there too.** Two refunded gifts and one written off, each
  recorded the way Open Impact records them, so you can see how a reversed gift behaves in
  the totals before it happens to you. One monthly donor is three payments in arrears, so the
  overdue installments have somebody in them.
- **People are connected to each other and to organizations.** 60 connections between people
  in different households and 80 affiliations, so the Relationships and Affiliations cards
  have something to show the first time you open a record.
- **Gifts are dated from the day you load.** The set always spans the last three years,
  whether you load it today or in two years, so "this year" is never empty and the retention
  reports always have renewals to count.
- **One action still removes all of it.** Remove Sample Data deletes the gifts, allocations,
  soft credits, tributes, funds, appeals, commitments, installments, connections and
  affiliations along with the households and people. Anything you entered yourself is
  untouched, and no sample gift is receipted, so nothing is left behind that cannot be
  deleted.
- **The card counts what it loaded.** The Sample Data card shows households, contacts,
  organizations, connections, affiliations and gifts, and a module that ships its own sample
  data in a later release adds its own number without the card changing.
- **One step to remember.** Giving totals (total giving, this year, largest gift, fund and
  appeal totals) are worked out by the rollup run rather than as each gift is saved. After
  loading, select Recalculate on the Rollups page, or wait for tonight, and the dashboard
  fills in.
