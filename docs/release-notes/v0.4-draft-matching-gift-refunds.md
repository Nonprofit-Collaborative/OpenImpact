# v0.4 draft release notes: a refunded match takes its recognition back (G-10, ADR-0031)

Draft lines for the v0.4 release notes, written for Maria rather than for developers (plan
Section 7.5). This closes the one defect `v0.3.md` left open under Open risks.

- **Refund a company's matching gift and the employee's recognition follows it down.** Until
  now the matched donor credit stood at the full amount however much of the match came back,
  so a donor could be recognized for 500 the company had already reversed. The refund now
  writes a negative matched donor credit for the same person, and the recognition total says
  what the company is still holding because of them.
- **Part of a match coming back takes part of the recognition with it.** A match of 500 with
  200 returned leaves the employee recognized for 300, not 500 and not nothing. Write-offs
  behave the same way as refunds.
- **Nothing is erased, which is how you can still explain last year's list.** The original
  credit stays on the company's gift at the amount that was matched, and the negative credit
  sits on the refund next to it. This is the same shape a refunded gift and a refunded
  household soft credit already had.
- **You no longer unlink the pair before recording a refund.** The old guidance said to,
  because the credit did not reverse on its own. The two gifts stay linked, which is the
  record of what the money was, and the recognition corrects itself. Open Impact still
  refuses to create a *new* link involving a gift that has been refunded or written off.
- **Refunding the employee's own gift leaves the match alone.** Their giving total drops,
  their recognition for the company's match does not, because the company's money is still
  money you are holding and they still caused it.
- **Nothing to turn on, and no new field.** The behavior arrives with the Giving module and
  the donor and household recognition totals you already have.

Admin guides: `docs/admin-guide/matching-gifts.md`, `docs/admin-guide/soft-credits.md`,
`docs/admin-guide/refunds.md`. Decision: ADR-0031.
