# v0.2 draft: gifts, funds, appeals, refunds

Release note lines for features G-01 (gifts, allocations, funds), G-04 (refunds and
write-offs) and G-05 (appeals). Written for Maria and David, not for developers. They are
collected into the iteration's release notes when v0.2 is promoted.

## New

- **Gifts.** Record what arrived, from whom, on what date, and what it was for. Gifts number
  themselves, so nobody types a name. See the [gifts guide](../admin-guide/gifts.md).
- **The household fills itself in.** Enter a gift from a person and the household credited with
  it is worked out for you, so household totals stay right when somebody marries, moves or is
  merged.
- **A date you can leave blank.** A gift saved without a date is today's gift, which is what you
  meant when you were entering a stack of checks.
- **Funds, and one default fund.** Set up the designations your organization uses, mark one as
  the default, and every gift entered without a designation lands there, so fund reporting is
  never missing money. See the [funds guide](../admin-guide/funds.md).
- **Allocations that always add up.** A gift's allocations total the gift exactly. Enter an
  amount or a percent and Open Impact works out the other one, putting any rounding difference
  on the largest allocation.
- **Appeals.** Name the efforts you want to measure, give them a goal, a cost and dates, and put
  one inside another where a gala sits within a campaign. Net raised and percent to goal are
  worked out as you look at them, so they are never stale. See the
  [appeals guide](../admin-guide/appeals.md).
- **Refunds and write-offs, without losing the original.** The **Refund or Write Off** button
  on a gift records money going back as a linked gift for a negative amount, with the reason on
  it and the original's designation mirrored, so the bank still reconciles and fund totals
  correct themselves. See the [refunds guide](../admin-guide/refunds.md).
- **A gift page that shows its own history.** Each gift lists its allocations and any refunds or
  write-offs against it, and gift allocations have a tab of their own.
- **Giving settings.** The Giving module now has its own settings page in the Nonprofit Settings
  console: default fund, default appeal, and automatic household soft credits.
- **Giving totals on donors, funds and appeals.** The fields the giving numbers are written to
  now exist on people, households, organizations, funds and appeals, each with a "rollups last
  calculated" stamp so you always know how fresh a number is.

## Changed

- Gifts, funds and appeals arrive with their own tabs, list views and layouts. Nothing needs to
  be built in Setup.

## Protected

- A gift with a receipt number cannot have its amount, date or donor changed, and the receipt
  number itself cannot be cleared or overwritten, so the lock cannot be lifted in two saves.
- A gift that carries a receipt or a refund cannot be deleted, and a fund that has received
  gifts cannot be deleted either: deactivate it instead.

## Known limits in this draft

- The totals themselves are calculated by the rollup engine (C-13), which lands separately in
  v0.2. Until it does, the giving total fields exist but stay empty.
- Splitting one gift across several funds is done on the gift entry screen (G-03), because
  allocations must always total the gift and the standard record page saves one row at a time.
- Receipts, acknowledgments and in-kind gift language arrive in v0.4. The fields they need ship
  now so that the gift object does not change under organizations that already hold data.
