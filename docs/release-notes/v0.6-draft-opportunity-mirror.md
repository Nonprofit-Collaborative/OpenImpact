# v0.6 draft release notes: Opportunity mirror

Draft lines for the v0.6 release notes, written for Maria (plan Section 7.5). Feature X-01, in
the Connect module.

- **Gifts and Opportunities, kept in step.** Choose **Gifts to Opportunities** in Nonprofit
  Settings and every gift gets a matching Opportunity, so NPSP rollups, Campaign totals and
  apps that only read Opportunities keep working. Choose **Opportunities to Gifts** instead when
  your online giving tool writes Opportunities, and each won Opportunity becomes a gift.
- **One direction at a time,** and off until you choose, whatever your org already has.
- **See where they disagree.** The new Opportunity mirror page compares gifts and won
  Opportunities for any date range, lists every difference, and puts the gift side right in one
  click.
- **Nothing is deleted, nothing is blocked.** A gift always saves, even when its Opportunity
  cannot be made; the reason is in the Error Log and the nightly run catches up. A gift made
  from an Opportunity is never changed by it afterwards, so a receipted gift stays as it was.
