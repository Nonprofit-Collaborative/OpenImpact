# v0.2 draft release notes: the guided Setup Assistant

Draft lines for the v0.2 release note, written for Maria.

- **Setup is now a guided path, not a list of homework.** The Nonprofit Hub opens on one
  step at a time, with Back, Next, and Skip for now. Each step explains itself, does its own
  work on the spot, and remembers where you got to: close the browser in the middle of step
  four and you come back to step four.
- **It knows what kind of org you have.** Step one says in plain words what it found,
  starting with the Nonprofit Cloud case ("Your org uses Person Accounts, as Nonprofit Cloud
  orgs do"), recommends how Open Impact should fit alongside it, and puts that in force when
  you confirm. Choose a different arrangement if you know better.
- **Households, access, and receipts are done in the assistant.** Confirm your household
  naming with the live preview, give a colleague a role, and record the legal name, tax
  identification number, address, logo, signature, and signer that your receipts are printed
  with, all without opening Salesforce Setup. Everything you record here is also on the
  Nonprofit Settings page afterwards, in a new **Organization** section.
- **Steps that need a module wait for that module.** Choosing your default fund and appeal
  says "Install the Giving module to choose defaults" until Giving is installed, and then
  offers the pickers. The last step opens quick gift entry when Giving is there and a "You
  are ready" summary when it is not. Nothing breaks on an org that has only Core.
- **An honest inventory of the modules.** Step six lists the six Open Impact packages with
  Present or Not installed and a link to what each one does. Turning a module on and off
  with one click arrives with the Module Manager in version 0.7, and the step says so.
- **It tells you how long it took.** When the last step is done the assistant shows the
  whole checklist and the elapsed time since your first change, then collapses to a small
  Setup complete tile with a Reopen setup button. Re-run it whenever your organization
  changes.
