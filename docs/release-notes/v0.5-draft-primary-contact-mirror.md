# v0.5 draft release notes: the household's primary contact, person accounts included

Draft lines for the v0.5 release notes, written for Maria rather than for developers
(plan Section 7.5). Feature C-22 (junction membership hardening with person accounts),
ADR-NEXT (primary contact mirrors the primary member).

- **Every household names its primary person, even when people are accounts.** In the
  flexible way of belonging, the household's Primary Contact now follows whoever is marked
  primary on its members. A person your org stores as an account (a person account) appears
  there under their own name. Before this, a household made only of person accounts had no
  primary contact at all.
- **Thank-you emails and reports see the same person.** Anything that reads Primary Contact,
  including acknowledgments for a gift given by a household, now finds the member you marked
  primary.
- **One place to change it.** Use **Make primary** on the household's **Household Members**
  panel. In the flexible way of belonging, typing a different Primary Contact on the household
  is refused with a message saying so, because the field would no longer match the members.
- **Nobody is promoted behind your back.** When the primary member leaves, Primary Contact is
  cleared and you choose the next one, the same as before.
- **The simple way of belonging is unchanged.** There, Primary Contact is still the field you
  set, and an organization's Primary Contact is yours to set by hand in either way.
