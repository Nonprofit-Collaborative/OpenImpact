# v0.5 draft release notes: multiple email addresses

Draft lines for the v0.5 release notes, written for Maria rather than for developers
(plan Section 7.5). Feature C-25 (multiple email addresses on a person).

- **Three email boxes instead of one.** Every person now has a personal email, a work email
  and an alternate email, next to the ordinary email box you already use. The addresses you
  have been keeping in a description field or a spreadsheet have somewhere to live.
- **Say which one to write to.** One choice, Preferred Email, says whether this person is
  reached at home, at work or at the third address. Set it and the ordinary email box is
  filled in from the address you chose.
- **Everything that sends email keeps working.** Acknowledgments, list views, reports,
  mass email, whatever tool you use: they all read the ordinary email box, and Open Impact
  keeps it pointed at the address you chose on every save. Nothing had to be taught about
  the new boxes.
- **Change the choice, not the address.** When somebody changes jobs you move one choice
  from Work to Personal. You do not retype an address, and you do not lose the old one: it
  is still in its own box if they go back.
- **An org that ignores this sees no change at all.** The copying only happens for a person
  who has a preferred email chosen. Leave the choice empty and the ordinary email box keeps
  exactly what you type in it, the way it always did.
- **It will not quietly cut somebody off.** Choosing an address you have not filled in yet
  refuses the save and tells you which box is empty, rather than emptying the ordinary email
  box. A person with no email address stops receiving everything, and nobody notices for
  months.
- **On the page where you already are.** The three boxes and the choice sit in an Email
  addresses section on the person's page, next to the ordinary email. There is nothing to
  turn on and nothing to set up in Setup.
- **The same on both kinds of org.** If your org stores people as accounts rather than as
  contacts, the three boxes and the choice are there too, and they keep that org's own
  person email in step in the same way.

## Stated plainly, what this release does not do

- **It does not remember which address you replaced.** Clearing the preferred email stops
  the copying, it does not put back whatever the ordinary email box held before. What was
  copied in last stays until somebody types over it.
- **It is not a list of addresses.** Three boxes, not an unlimited history, which is what a
  small nonprofit actually needs. If you need a fourth, put it in the alternate box.
- **It does not check whether an address still works.** Bounce handling is your email tool's
  job, not Open Impact's.
