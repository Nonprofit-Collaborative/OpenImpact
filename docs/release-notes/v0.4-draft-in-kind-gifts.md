# v0.4 draft release notes: in-kind gifts

Draft lines for the v0.4 release notes, written for Maria rather than for developers
(plan Section 7.5). Feature G-18 (in-kind gifts with fair market value and description).

- **Record what arrived, not what it cost you.** A gift of goods or services is entered as a
  gift like any other, with the type **In-kind**, plus two things only it has: a description
  of what arrived and a fair market value for your own reporting.
- **Quick Gift Entry knows the difference.** Choose In-kind and the amount box is replaced by
  a description box and a fair market value box, with the description required, because the
  description is what your donor's receipt will say.
- **In-kind gifts never touch your cash numbers.** An in-kind gift carries no amount, so it
  adds nothing to total giving, to a gift count, to a fund or appeal total, to your dashboard
  or to a donor level. Your development report and your finance report agree about what came
  in as cash, which is the thing that used to take an afternoon to reconcile.
- **They do have numbers of their own.** Two new totals, **In-kind value** and **In-kind gift
  count**, sit next to total giving on every household, organization and person, maintained
  by the same rollup engine as everything else. Total giving is cash, in-kind value is goods,
  and they are deliberately not added together.
- **A receipt for goods describes and never values.** Where a cash receipt prints an amount,
  an in-kind receipt says that no value is stated, and it carries the sentence that names what
  you received and says plainly that your organization has not valued it. Valuing donated
  property is the donor's job, not yours, and there is no setting that can put your figure on
  their receipt.
- **On a year-end statement, an in-kind gift is a line with no amount**, outside the total the
  statement adds up, so a donor can see the goods they gave without the statement claiming a
  deduction on their behalf.
- **The description is locked once a receipt is issued**, exactly as the amount is on a cash
  gift, because it is printed on the document the donor is holding. The fair market value
  stays editable, because it is on no document.
- **The app stops the mistake that matters.** An in-kind gift with an amount typed into it is
  refused, and so is one saved without a description, and so is a cash gift left carrying an
  in-kind value after somebody changed its type.
- **A new report, In-kind Gifts This Year**, lists gifts of goods with their descriptions and
  values, which is the list your finance team asks for at year end.
- **Nothing to turn on.** In-kind has always been one of the gift types; this release makes it
  behave correctly everywhere. The sample data includes about twenty in-kind gifts to look at.
