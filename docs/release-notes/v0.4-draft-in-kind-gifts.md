# v0.4 draft release notes: in-kind gifts (G-18)

Draft lines for the v0.4 release notes, written for Maria rather than for developers (plan
Section 7.5). Feature G-18, which finishes what the receipting work started.

- **A gift of goods is recorded as goods.** Set a gift's type to In-kind, describe what was
  given, and record what your organization values it at. Open Impact will not let you save an
  in-kind gift without both, because a gift of goods that says neither what it was nor what it
  was worth is not a record of anything.
- **One gift, one number.** The amount and the fair market value of an in-kind gift are the
  same figure, and Open Impact refuses a gift where they disagree. Two numbers for one gift is
  two answers to "what did we receive", and the reports would eventually give both.
- **The receipt describes the gift and never prices it.** Where your letter asks for an
  amount, an in-kind receipt says "the goods or services described below". The description you
  typed is printed underneath, with the sentence saying that your organization has not placed
  a value on the gift. In the United States, valuing donated property is the donor's job, and
  a charity that puts a number on it has handed the donor a problem.
- **A year-end statement lists gifts of goods without a value, outside the total, and says
  so.** The line carries its date, its type and its status, the amount column reads "Not
  valued", the total at the bottom is the money only, and one sentence explains the
  difference, so a donor who adds the lines up is not left wondering which number is wrong.
- **A gift of money cannot carry an in-kind description.** Open Impact refuses that too. It
  would produce a receipt describing goods that nobody gave.
- **Refunds work exactly as they do for money.** Use Refund or Write off on the original gift.
  The negative gift names the gift it reverses and is not asked to describe the goods again.
- **In-kind gifts count in your giving totals**, at the value you recorded, because the
  packaged totals count gifts by status and not by type. If you report cash separately, filter
  a report on Type. This is stated here rather than left to be discovered on a board report.

Most of what this feature needed was already in the product: the In-kind type, the two fields,
and the receipt sentence shipped with receipting (G-13). What v0.4 adds is the four rules that
stop a wrong in-kind gift being saved, and the removal of the one thing an in-kind document
could still do wrong, which was printing an amount where the letter asked for one.
