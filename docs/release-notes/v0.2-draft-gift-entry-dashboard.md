# v0.2 draft release note lines: quick gift entry and the Giving dashboard

**Status:** draft, for G-03 and G-06. These lines fold into `v0.2.md` when the iteration
is written up (plan Section 9.5), and into the admin-facing notes that ship with the
package version (plan Section 7.5). Written for Maria and David, not for developers.

## New

- **Enter a gift in one screen, from your phone.** The Fundraising app opens on Quick Gift
  Entry: choose the donor, type the amount, pick how the gift arrived, and save. Today's
  date, your default appeal and your default fund are filled in already, and Save and new
  keeps them so a stack of checks goes in one after another. Every gift is confirmed by
  name with a link to open it, and a gift that is already saved cannot be saved a second
  time by tapping again on a slow connection.
- **A donor can be a person or an organization.** The form asks which, and in an org where
  people are accounts it searches accounts for people, so nobody has to know how their org
  is set up.
- **A new Fundraising app.** Gifts, Funds, Appeals, Quick Gift Entry, Dashboards and
  Reports in one place, next to the Nonprofit Hub in the app launcher.
- **Six giving reports and a dashboard, ready to read.** Gifts This Month by Day, Giving by
  Month (the last twelve months), Giving by Fund This Year, Giving by Appeal This Year, Top
  Donors This Year and Recent Gifts, in a shared Open Impact Giving folder, with a Giving
  Overview dashboard built on them. The filters are relative, so nothing needs editing after
  installation, this month or next year.
- **Two report types you can build your own reports from:** Gifts, and Gifts with
  Allocations for anything that has to be counted by fund.

## What to do once, after installing

- **Choose a default fund and a default appeal** in Nonprofit Settings, so that gifts
  entered in a hurry still land in the right place. Quick Gift Entry shows the fund the
  gift will actually be designated to, whether that comes from your settings or from the
  fund you marked as the default.
- **Give people access.** Assign the Fundraising Staff permission set group to the people
  who enter gifts, and Read Only to board members who read the dashboard. The Giving
  Overview dashboard reads as whoever is looking at it, so each person sees the gifts they
  are allowed to see, and nothing else needs setting.

## Known limits in this version

- The Giving dashboard is opened from the Dashboards tab in the Fundraising app rather than
  from a tab of its own: a packaged Lightning page cannot point at a packaged dashboard
  (ADR-0018).
- Top Donors groups by household. Gifts from companies and foundations have no household and
  are counted in the fund and appeal reports instead.
- Quick Gift Entry saves one gift at a time. Entering twenty checks against a control total
  is the gift batch grid, which arrives in v0.5 (G-17).
