# v0.3 draft release notes: commitments

Draft lines for the v0.3 release notes, written for Maria rather than for developers
(plan Section 7.5). Features G-07 (commitments) and G-11 (commitment and pledge balance
rollup definitions).

- **Pledges and recurring gifts live in one place.** Record what a donor promised, how
  often they pay it, and when it starts, and Open Impact works out the payments that are
  expected and when each one is due.
- **The payments are real records, so you can report on them.** The commitment page shows
  the whole schedule with what has been paid against each payment.
- **Overdue and Upcoming 30 days are shipped list views.** Jen opens Overdue to see what
  has not arrived, and Upcoming 30 days to see what should.
- **Active pledges and Active recurring are shipped list views on commitments**, so
  David can see the two kinds of promise apart without building a filter.
- **A gift pays a payment.** Enter a gift against a commitment and the payment it belongs
  to is marked Paid or Partially paid and the balance drops. A gift that names the
  commitment but not a particular payment goes to the oldest unpaid one by itself, which
  you can turn off in settings.
- **Pause, resume, and cancel without losing history.** Pausing stops new payments being
  created and leaves the ones already scheduled alone. Cancelling marks the unpaid ones
  Skipped. Nothing that has been received is ever changed or deleted.
- **A pledge completes itself** once the gifts against it reach the promised total.
- **Recurring gifts never fill the org with rows.** A year of payments is kept in front
  of an open-ended schedule (you choose how far ahead), extended by a nightly job that
  also marks payments overdue after a grace period you set.
- **Pledge balance for donors.** The balance of a donor's active pledges rolls up to the
  household, the organization, and the person, alongside the giving totals.
- **Four settings, all in the app**, in Nonprofit Settings under Giving: the generation
  horizon, the overdue grace days, whether gifts are applied to payments automatically,
  and a read-only stamp showing when the nightly job last ran.
