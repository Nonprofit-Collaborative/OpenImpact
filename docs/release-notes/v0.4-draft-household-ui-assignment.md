# v0.4 draft release notes: the household pages arrive already wired up

Draft lines for the v0.4 release notes, written for Maria rather than for developers
(plan Section 7.5). Features C-01, C-02, C-08 and C-09, defect found by reading what a
fresh install actually shows.

- **Households, organizations and household contacts open on their own pages.** In the
  Nonprofit Hub app, a household now opens on the page Open Impact ships: the members
  panel, the address card and the record details, on the desktop and on the phone. Before,
  every one of those records opened on the plain Salesforce page and the panels were
  nowhere to be seen, so the household walkthrough stopped at step 4 until somebody rebuilt
  the page by hand in the Lightning App Builder.
- **The Merge or split button is on the household page.** It sits in the button bar at the
  top of a household, next to Edit, with nothing to place first.
- **Gifts and commitments open on their own pages too**, in the Fundraising app, for the
  same reason.

## Stated plainly, what this release does not do

- **Open Impact does not touch a page you have already changed.** If somebody has assigned
  their own record page for households, that assignment is theirs and it wins. The pages
  Open Impact ships are still there to switch back to.
- **The assignments are per app.** Households opened from an app other than Nonprofit Hub,
  including a custom app of your own, show whatever record page that app assigns.
