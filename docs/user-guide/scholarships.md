# Scholarships and Grants

Scholarships and Outbound Grants tracks the money your organization gives: applications, reviews,
awards, payments and the reports recipients owe. Your admin turns it on; the setup is in the Admin
Guide's [Scholarships and Outbound Grants](../admin-guide/scholarships.md) page.

## The Scholarships and Grants Page

Open the **BarnCRM Scholarships** app (or **BarnCRM** or **BarnCRM Console**) and its
**Scholarships and Grants** tab. Before your organization's first award program, it
opens with **Set Up Your First Award Program** and a **New Award Program** button. It shows:

- **My Reviews**: applications assigned to you that you have not submitted. Open one to review it.
- **Open Cycles**: cycles taking applications or in review, with their close dates and how many
  applications they have.
- **Disbursements Due**: payments scheduled in the next 30 days, with any that are held back by a
  condition.
- **Export Disbursements** (for managers): pick a date range and download a CSV for your
  accounting system.

## Reviewing an Application

1. Open the application. The **Review** panel shows the cycle's rubric.
2. Give each criterion a score (up to its highest) and add comments. The panel shows the weighted
   total as you go.
3. Press **Save** to come back later, or **Submit Review** when you are done. Only submitted
   reviews count.
4. If you have a conflict of interest (you know the applicant, for example), press **Recuse**,
   give a short reason and save. A manager can then assign someone else.

With blind review on, the panel shows the application number instead of the applicant's name.

## The Review Board and Decisions

Open a cycle. The **Review Board** lists its applications ranked by average score, with how many
reviews are in, the amount requested and the budget left. Managers (Manage Scholarships) can:

- press **Assign Reviewers** to share the applications out among the cycle's reviewer panel;
- press **Award** on an application, check the amount and save, which creates an Offered award;
- press **Decline** to mark it declined.

## Awards

On an award:

- **Make Letter** creates the award letter as a PDF in the award's Files. Download it and send it.
- **Accepted** or **Declined** records the recipient's answer (they can also accept in the
  applicant portal).
- **Build Schedule** creates the payments: how many and how many months apart.
- **Start Renewal** (renewable awards) starts next cycle's application for the same recipient.

## Disbursements and Conditions

Each payment shows whether a condition holds it back. Pay it in your accounting system, then press
**Mark Paid** and enter the date, method and reference. BarnCRM does not send money.

Conditions and reports are on the award's Award Requirements list. When the recipient has met a
condition, press **Met**; a manager can **Waive** one. A held-back payment can be marked Paid once
its conditions are Met or Waived.

## Recipient Reports

Progress and final reports are requirements with due dates. The owner of the award's program gets a
reminder before each is due. When the recipient sends a report through their report link it shows as
Submitted with their answers and files; review it and press **Met**.
